# Pipeline Architecture v4.x — Moving-Camera Extension (deferred)

> **Status:** Sub-spec, deferred behind the main robustness audit. Pick up after the parent's Waves 1-2 land and a moving-camera capture mode is on the roadmap.
> **Parent:** [`2026-05-11-arch-4.x-changes-design.md`](./2026-05-11-arch-4.x-changes-design.md)
> **Date:** 2026-05-11
> **Scope:** Extensions to v4.x for scenarios where the camera moves during a capture (pan, tilt, dolly across a fixed card layout, or mixed camera + card motion). Out of scope of the main audit, which assumes a stationary camera and only-moving cards.

---

## 0. Premise

The parent spec assumes a fixed camera over a workspace where cards enter and leave. This describes the dominant capture setup (tripod or phone holder over a flat surface) and is the right baseline for the next several releases.

This sub-spec covers two scenarios the parent intentionally does not handle:

1. **Tour-the-binder.** The user holds a phone and pans across a fixed layout — binder pages, a sorted row, a display case. Cards never move; the camera does.
2. **Hybrid.** The camera moves occasionally during a capture session while cards are also being placed and lifted.

When the camera moves, four v4.1 mechanisms degrade in ways that the parent spec's fixed-camera fixes do not address:

- The background model is invalidated the moment the camera moves — workspace pixels shift under a model that assumed they were stationary.
- `CentroidJumpDetector` fires on pan-induced bbox motion and forces a spurious session reset, even though the card itself never moved relative to the workspace.
- Sobel-valley detection collapses: camera motion produces global edge change that drowns the per-card-swap signal.
- The "card moved" signal is conflated with "camera moved", so no session-split signal can be defined in terms of card displacement alone.

These are real failure modes, but only for users who actually move the camera. The decision to defer is a scope decision, not a correctness decision.

---

## 1. Failure mode (detailed)

Concrete v4.1 mechanisms that break under camera motion:

- **`BackgroundModel.gray`** (`presence/background_novelty.py:31`) is a single per-pixel mean built from sampler proxies, never updated against camera motion. A pan shifts the entire workspace under the model, so every per-quad novelty score becomes either uniformly elevated (gate fires on everything) or the gate has to be loosened to the point of uselessness.
- **`CentroidJumpDetector`** (`tracking/centroid_jump.py`) thresholds on `|cx − recent_cx| > jump_ratio · frame_width` (default `0.30 · width`). A camera pan that drags a stationary card across the frame trivially exceeds this threshold and forces a session reset.
- **Sobel-valley split detection** in `sampler/valley_splits.py` reduces the proxy frame to a single scalar `sobel_score`. A camera move produces a globally-elevated Sobel signal with no valley, masking any per-card swap signal that occurs during the move.
- **The four session-split signals** (sampler frame-index gap, valley split, centroid jump, BoT-SORT ReID shift) all carry no concept of "the card moved relative to the workspace". There is no signal that survives a moving camera.

Inner-region occlusion (proposal #14 of the parent spec, the per-tile appearance-residual mechanism) is *not* in this sub-spec — that work is occluder-agnostic and motion-agnostic, useful regardless of camera state. It stays in the parent.

## 2. Proposals

### Proposal MC-1 — Camera ego-motion estimation (foundational)

**What.** Between consecutive scan frames (or detection frames; see open questions §4), estimate a 2D affine transform `H_t` from feature correspondences *outside* current card-track regions. Sparse Lucas-Kanade on Shi-Tomasi corners under a card-region mask is the primary path; whole-frame ECC (`cv2.findTransformECC` with `MOTION_AFFINE`) is the fallback when LK correspondences are too few.

**Why.** Every other proposal in this sub-spec depends on knowing how much the camera moved between frames. Without this signal nothing else here is implementable.

**Impact.** High in any moving-camera scenario; this is the foundation. Neutral when the camera is stationary (the estimator returns ~identity).

**Effort.** M (~150 LoC including a confidence signal for "estimation unreliable on this frame" — falls back to assumed-identity when confidence is low).

**Compute.** ~1 ms CPU per scan frame on a 192-px proxy with ~100 LK points; ECC fallback is ~5 ms per frame.

**Memory.** ~negligible per-frame state (the previous frame's grayscale proxy and feature set).

**Stages touched.** 1 (sampler context), 5 (tracker).

**Confidence handling.** Emit `(H_t, confidence)`; downstream proposals MC-2 through MC-4 must consume both and degrade to v4.1 behavior when `confidence < 0.5`. Conditions for low confidence include: fewer than ~20 LK correspondences survived the forward-backward check, ECC fallback failed to converge, or the residual after applying `H_t` is large.

### Proposal MC-2 — Motion-warped background model

**What.** Warp the BG model (the parent spec's per-pixel mean and variance from Proposal #10, or the v4.1 single mean if Proposal #10 has not landed) by `H_t^{-1}` before differencing in `quad_novelty`. Skip BG update during high-motion periods — warp error scales with motion magnitude and an updated BG built from warped frames compounds error.

**Why.** A panning camera no longer invalidates the novelty gate. The gate sees a coherent background regardless of camera state.

**Impact.** High in camera-mobile scenarios; neutral when the camera is stationary (warp is identity).

**Effort.** S after MC-1 (~50 LoC, one `cv2.warpAffine` call per gated frame).

**Compute.** Sub-ms warp per gated frame at 1080p; ~negligible.

**Memory.** 0.

**Stages touched.** 4.

**Composes with parent Proposal #10.** When per-pixel BG (mean + variance) has landed, warp both channels.

### Proposal MC-3 — Ego-motion-corrected centroid-jump

**What.** Before computing `|cx − recent_cx|` in `CentroidJumpDetector`, subtract the camera-explained displacement (apply `H_t^{-1}` to the previous centroid). Trigger on residual jump only.

**Why.** A pan that drags a stationary card across the frame no longer forces a session reset.

**Impact.** High in camera-mobile scenarios; neutral when stationary.

**Effort.** S after MC-1 (~20 LoC).

**Compute.** Negligible.

**Memory.** 0.

**Stages touched.** 5.

### Proposal MC-4 — Card residual motion as session-split signal

**What.** Add a 5th session-reset condition: the per-track bbox centroid's *residual motion* (after subtracting the camera-explained displacement from MC-1) exceeds a threshold within a short window of frames. Distinct from MC-3, which fires on a *single-frame* residual jump — this one fires on accumulated residual over a window.

**Why.** A card-lift-and-replace performed during a pan with no time gap, where the new card occupies a slightly different rectified position from the old card, produces accumulated residual motion of the tracked region. None of v4.1's four existing signals catch this — sampler-gap requires a time gap, valley-split needs a Sobel valley masked by camera motion, the centroid-jump is camera-confounded (until MC-3), and ReID-shift is currently broken (parent Proposal #1).

**Impact.** Med-High in camera-mobile scenarios.

**Effort.** S after MC-1 (~50 LoC, mostly the windowed accumulator and threshold).

**Compute.** Negligible.

**Memory.** Per-track windowed centroid history (~16 floats × N tracks); negligible.

**Stages touched.** 5.

## 3. Risks and open questions

- **Ego-motion needs non-card features.** An extreme close-up where the entire frame is filled by one card has no usable correspondences. The estimator's confidence signal must drive a clean fallback to v4.1 behavior; failing silently to identity-on-low-confidence is correct but should be measured (false-positive rate of "low confidence" should be small in moving-camera captures).
- **Affine vs. homography.** Affine is sufficient for pan/tilt with a far-field workspace; homography matters for close-range camera moves where parallax kicks in. Start with affine; reconsider if regression metrics on tour-the-binder captures show parallax errors dominating.
- **Camera shake vs. intentional pan.** Both produce ego-motion. The downstream signals don't care about intent — they care about magnitude — so shake just adds a small-amplitude `H_t` that gets warped against. The motion-warped BG model will see slightly noisier residuals but no qualitative regression. Worth confirming on a hand-held capture.
- **Cadence.** Run ego-motion on every scan frame (~15 fps), every detection frame (post-sampler, sparser), or both? Denser cadence gives the BG model better following ability during fast moves; sparser cadence is cheaper. Tentatively: every scan frame, since LK on a 192-px proxy is cheap.
- **No measurement infrastructure yet.** Until the parent spec's Proposal #9 (robustness metric pack) lands and includes a "moving-camera scene" tag, there is no automated way to know if these proposals are working. This sub-spec is *unmeasurable* until the parent spec's Wave 2 ships. That's intentional — there's no point shipping moving-camera robustness without the metric to verify it.

## 4. Sequencing and prerequisites

These four proposals form a single dependent chain:
- MC-1 is the foundation; the other three are S effort after it lands.
- MC-2, MC-3, MC-4 are independent of each other and can ship in any order once MC-1 lands.

Prerequisites from the parent spec (recommended before starting this sub-spec):
- **Parent #9 (robustness metric pack), with a "moving-camera" scene tag.** Without this, none of MC-1 through MC-4 can be verified.
- **Parent #10 (per-pixel BG mean + variance).** MC-2 composes naturally with this; without it, MC-2 still works against the v4.1 single-mean BG but the variance term is unavailable.

Wave 1 (MC sub-spec): MC-1.
Wave 2 (MC sub-spec): MC-2, MC-3, MC-4 in parallel.

Total effort estimate: ~1 week of focused work after parent Waves 1-2 are merged.
