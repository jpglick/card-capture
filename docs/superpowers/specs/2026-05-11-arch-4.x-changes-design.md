# Pipeline Architecture v4.x — Proposed Changes

> **Status:** High-level change document, revised after review feedback. To be refined into per-change specs and an integrated plan.
> **Baseline:** [`arch-4.1.md`](../../../arch-4.1.md), commit `f9e69990`.
> **Date:** 2026-05-11.
> **Scope:** Robustness audit of the v4.1 pipeline under a **stationary-camera assumption** — the camera is mounted over a workspace and cards enter/leave; the camera itself does not move during a capture. Moving-camera robustness (pan-the-binder, hybrid moves) is deferred to a sub-spec at [`2026-05-11-arch-4.x-moving-camera-design.md`](./2026-05-11-arch-4.x-moving-camera-design.md).
> **Constraints:** make as few scene assumptions as possible within the fixed-camera baseline — no reliance on hands or any other agent identification.
> **ML budget:** no new training and no new pretrained models; algorithmic fixes only, with parameter recalibration permitted.
> **Forward goal:** every change should be compatible with an eventual single-pass / real-time pipeline (see §8.4).

---

## 0. Synthesis

The v4.1 pipeline is competent and well-instrumented, but five structural weaknesses recur across the stage-by-stage critiques in `arch-4.1.md`:

1. **Photometric variation is treated as noise**, not as a modelable signal. The background model is a single grayscale mean; the glare term is a saturated-pixel fraction with no spatial structure; the median fusion presumes pixel-perfect alignment that the corner detector does not deliver; foils are unmodeled.
2. **Inner-region occlusion is invisible.** The `border_purity` term (added `55c79a04`) catches anything crossing the card's outer 3% ring but is blind to occlusions inside the rectified crop — a fingertip resting on the player photo without crossing the border, a stray sticker, lens flare, or the edge of an adjacent card poking in. There is no agent-agnostic mechanism to detect localized appearance changes in the interior of a tracked card.
3. **Multi-card and rotational scenes lose information at every junction.** The tracker reduces 4-corner OBBs to axis-aligned envelopes; the centroid-jump detector uses bbox centers; valley detection collapses the frame to a single scalar.
4. **Identity confusion is the highest-leverage failure surface.** BoT-SORT's appearance branch is fed `np.zeros((480, 640, 3))` at `botsort_adapter.py:137`, so OSNet ReID is effectively disabled. The `side_score` (textiness) is computed at `pipeline.py:583` and never read. The Front/Back gate compares pHashes (`pipeline.py:936`) — the wrong invariant for distinguishing two sides of the same physical card.
5. **Thresholds are hand-tuned and untethered to outcome metrics.** A regression-truth framework exists at `tests/regression/` but isn't wired to threshold tuning.

This document proposes changes organized as five capability themes. Each theme names the failure mode, lists concrete proposals, and rates them on a uniform impact/effort scale. A flat triage table at the end (§6) reorders the same proposals for sequencing.

**Per-proposal fields:** WHAT (the change) · WHY (why it helps) · IMPACT (high / med / low) · EFFORT (S / M / L) · Retraining (none / threshold sweep / small head) · Stages touched.

## 1. Theme — Photometric variation (lighting drift, glare, foils)

### Failure mode

`BackgroundModel.gray` (`presence/background_novelty.py:31`) is a single per-pixel mean of N "empty" frames; it carries no variance. `quad_novelty` (`:80-104`) converts the frame to grayscale before differencing — color information is discarded. A workspace under flickering light, autofocus pumping, or shifting exposure inflates the per-pixel residual uniformly and softens the gate.

The glare term in `QualityScorer.score` and the lighting-diverse selection in `MultiFrameFuser.select_lighting_diverse_indices` both threshold on `gray > 200`. A 5-pixel specular highlight and a 5,000-pixel blown-out player photo are treated identically. The fusion step is a per-pixel median over selected frames; for foils — where the underlying card content genuinely shifts between frames — the median washes out the holographic look rather than preserving it.

### Proposals

1. **Per-pixel BG model with variance.** Replace `BackgroundModel.gray: (H, W)` with `(mean, variance): (H, W) × 2`, EWMA-updated from sampler-detected empty windows. Novelty becomes a Mahalanobis-style score `clip((|frame - mean| - k·sqrt(var)) / 255, 0, 1)`. **Impact:** High. **Effort:** M. **Retraining:** Threshold sweep. **Stages:** 1, 4.

2. **Lab-color novelty.** Store and difference `L, a, b` channels with weights `(1.0, 0.5, 0.5)` rather than grayscale. **Why:** A card whose back matches the table in luminance but differs in chroma is invisible to the current gate (e.g., a tan card on a wooden workspace). **Impact:** Med-High. **Effort:** S. **Retraining:** Threshold sweep. **Stages:** 4.

3. **Periodic BG refresh during sampler-detected empty stretches.** The sampler already identifies inter-window gaps with no card present (`last_inter_window_gaps_frames`). Use these to update the BG model mid-video rather than freezing it after Stage 1. **Why:** Long captures (10+ min) drift; a single initial BG goes stale. **Impact:** Med (long-capture-specific). **Effort:** S. **Retraining:** None. **Stages:** 1 → 4.

4. **Spatial-glare metric.** Replace "fraction of pixels > 240" with a connected-component analysis: penalize the *largest contiguous saturated blob's area*, optionally weighted higher if the blob's centroid is within the central 60% of the canvas (the photo region on most TCG cards). **Why:** Scattered specular vs. a single blowout currently score the same. **Impact:** Med-High. **Effort:** S. **Retraining:** Recalibrate the scoring weight. **Stages:** 7, 9.

5. **ECC re-registration before median fusion.** Within a track's `_CANONICAL_TARGET_FRAMES` selected frames, run `cv2.findTransformECC` (translation or Euclidean) on each non-reference frame against the reference, apply the warp, then median. **Why:** The corner detector wobbles ±1-3 px frame-to-frame (arch §9.3); the current median ghosts edges. **Impact:** Med — directly improves the asset the user sees. **Effort:** S-M (needs convergence-failure fallback). **Retraining:** None. **Stages:** 9.

6. **Foil-aware fusion path.** Detect foils per-track by computing the spatial variance of the high-pass Laplacian energy across selected canonical frames; when the high-frequency mass *moves between frames* (holographic surface), switch from median to a glare-rejection-only fusion ("pick the frame whose pixel is closest to the per-pixel median" — preserves card luminance, rejects bright outliers). **Why:** Median fusion presumes only glare moves between frames; foils invert this. **Impact:** Med (foil-specific; high subjective value to TCG users). **Effort:** M. **Retraining:** None initially; a learned foil classifier on cards.sqlite is a follow-up. **Stages:** 9.

### Risks & open questions
- A per-pixel BG model is sensitive to boot conditions: a "background proxy" frame that actually contains a card poisons the relevant pixels. The sampler's lowest-presence-score heap is good but not perfect — add a sanity check (e.g., reject any proxy whose Sobel mean is in the top quartile of all proxies).
- Lab conversion is ~one extra `cv2.cvtColor` per frame in the gate path; cost is negligible but worth measuring.
- ECC fails to converge on near-uniform card backs (no gradient); the fallback path must be explicit, not silent.

## 2. Theme — Occluder-agnostic occlusion detection

### Failure mode

`_border_purity_score` in `scoring.py` (added `55c79a04`, weighted 20% in the quality score) compares the outer 3% ring's std-dev against the interior std-dev. It is a strong cue when an occluder *crosses the border* of the rectified card — the most common failure mode — but it is blind in three concrete situations:

- **Inner-only occlusions.** A fingertip resting on the player-photo region of a flat card, a sticker placed mid-card, lens flare in the middle of the crop. None disturb the outer ring; none are detected.
- **Adjacent-card edge.** When a neighboring card lies near enough that its edge appears inside the rectified crop without crossing the outer ring (more common at oblique angles, where rectification pulls in nearby pixels).
- **Localized photometric artifacts.** A specular highlight that's bright enough to lose the underlying card content in one region but not enough to trigger the global glare term.

These all share a property: a *localized* region of the rectified card appears anomalous, while the rest of the card is fine. v4.1 has no mechanism keyed on that pattern.

### Proposals

1. **Per-tile appearance-residual occlusion detection.** For each tracked card, maintain a per-tile (e.g., 5×7 grid on the 750×1050 rectified canvas, ~150×150 px tiles) running median of pixel values across the track's recent frames (e.g., last 16). Per new frame, compute `|current_tile − median_tile|`; a *contiguous connected blob* of high-residual tiles indicates a localized occlusion. Use as: (a) per-frame quality penalty in `QualityScorer`, (b) optional candidate gate at Stage 4 when the occluded area exceeds a threshold. **Why:** Agent-agnostic — fingers, neighboring cards, stickers, lens artifacts, foil shifts, all behave the same way at the appearance level. Inner-region occlusions that `border_purity` misses are caught here. **Impact:** Med-High. **Effort:** M (~120 LoC). **Retraining:** None. **Stages:** 7 (quality term); optional 4 (gate). **Compute:** ~2 ms per tracked card per frame for K=35 tiles and a 16-frame median window.

### Risks & open questions

- Per-tile residual depends on stable rectification. The corner detector wobbles ±1-3 px frame-to-frame (arch §9.3), which would otherwise produce elevated residuals at every edge tile. Compose this with Proposal #12 (ECC re-registration) — that proposal aligns the canonical-fusion frames; this proposal needs the per-frame analogue: align each new frame against the track's running median before computing the residual.
- A card flipped from Front to Back is, by definition, a large whole-card appearance residual. The occlusion signal must suppress when the high-residual region is *whole-card* rather than localized — that's the Front→Back transition the existing pipeline handles in Stage 8. Easy rule: only treat as occlusion if the high-residual connected blob covers less than ~30% of tiles.
- Boot conditions: a track has no median to compare against in its first few frames. Either accumulate silently for the first K frames (no signal until then) or seed from the canonical view of a similar prior track (more complex; probably not worth the indirection).

> **Cross-reference:** Camera-motion-induced false positives (a pan that shifts what counts as "the same card position" frame-to-frame) are *not* in scope of this theme. Moving-camera handling is deferred to [`2026-05-11-arch-4.x-moving-camera-design.md`](./2026-05-11-arch-4.x-moving-camera-design.md).

## 3. Theme — Multi-card and rapid-swap scenes

### Failure mode

The tracker adapters reduce each 4-corner OBB to an axis-aligned `xyxy` envelope (`_xyxy_from_corners`). Two cards in the same frame at 90° to each other can produce overlapping envelopes and confuse the association. `CentroidJumpDetector` operates on bbox centers — a card rotated in-place produces a moving envelope center even though the OBB center is stationary, spuriously triggering session resets. `find_valley_splits` reduces the proxy frame to a single Sobel scalar; a swap where card A leaves on the left as card B enters on the right produces a roughly flat scalar and no valley. The `adaptive_min_track_length` formula `len(detection_rows) // 3` (`pipeline.py:250` approx) overshoots on videos where a single card dominates a long stretch.

### Proposals

1. **Oriented-IoU association.** Implement `rotated_iou(quad_a, quad_b)` (Shapely or a small custom routine) and substitute it for axis-aligned IoU inside the adapter wrapper around boxmot. **Why:** Two cards at 90° to each other become correctly disjoint. **Impact:** High in multi-card scenes; neutral elsewhere. **Effort:** M (depends on what boxmot exposes; may require a custom association step rather than an override). **Retraining:** None. **Stages:** 5.

2. **OBB centroid for centroid-jump.** Replace bbox-center with the polygon centroid (mean of the 4 corners). **Why:** In-place rotation no longer triggers spurious resets. **Impact:** Med. **Effort:** S. **Retraining:** None. **Stages:** 5.

3. **Per-region valley detection.** Tile the proxy frame into a 3×3 grid; compute per-tile Sobel-valley signals; emit a split if *any* tile's valley persists ≥ `valley_min_width_frames`. **Why:** A "card A leaves left, card B enters right" swap with no global Sobel valley is currently missed. **Impact:** Med-High in multi-card / fluid-swap captures. **Effort:** M. **Retraining:** Threshold sweep. **Stages:** 1.

4. **Inter-detection-gap-derived min track length.** Replace `len(detection_rows) // 3` with `max(3, expected_session_length_frames / median_inter_detection_gap)`. **Why:** Removes a known pathology (long single-card videos getting their threshold made artificially harder). **Impact:** Med. **Effort:** S. **Retraining:** None. **Stages:** 5.

### Risks & open questions
- Oriented IoU is correct but ~10× more expensive than axis-aligned. At 16 detections per batch this is still trivial (microseconds), but a regression check on detection-heavy frames is prudent.
- Per-region valley adds 9× the threshold-sweep dimension; the auto-sweep harness in Theme 5 needs to handle it.

## 4. Theme — Identity confusion (Front/Back, ReID, dedup)

The highest-leverage section of this document. Three load-bearing failures, all confirmed in code.

### Failure mode

1. **BoT-SORT ReID is disabled.** `botsort_adapter.py:137` constructs `np.zeros((480, 640, 3), dtype=np.uint8)` and passes it as the image argument to `tracker.update`. The OSNet `osnet_x0_25_msmt17` ReID backbone runs on this dummy image for every detection. Every embedding, every `pending_splits` decision, and the `reid_distance_threshold = 0.6` operate on degraded noise. The comment at `:136` acknowledges this.

2. **`side_score` is computed and discarded.** Defined at `_PreparedTrack.side_score: float` (`pipeline.py:72`), computed at `pipeline.py:583` via `_side_textiness_score(best_canonical["normalized"])`. Two recursive greps confirm there is no other reference. The textiness signal — high for image-rich fronts, low for uniform-color backs — is exactly the right invariant for Front/Back assignment and is currently dead code.

3. **The Front/Back gate uses the wrong invariant.** `_resolve_session_tracks` at `pipeline.py:908-941` sorts tracks by length, calls the longest "Front", and admits a second track as "Back" only if `hamming_distance(front_phash, other_phash) ≤ _SAME_CARD_HAMMING_MAX = 22` (`pipeline.py:50`). A pHash matches when *content and contour* are similar. Front and back of the same physical card share contour but differ in content — the gate works only because the 22/64 (~34%) threshold is loose enough to admit very different content, which simultaneously admits genuinely different cards (false-positive same-card calls).

4. **Primary-track selection is length-not-quality.** `session_tracks.sort(key=lambda item: len(item[1].track.candidates), reverse=True)` at `pipeline.py:924`. A short sharp front-view loses to a long blurry back-view.

### Proposals

1. **⭐ Pass real frames to BoT-SORT.** Plumb the source frame through `ScoredCandidate` (or a parallel structure) and pass it to `tracker.update(det, frame_bgr)` instead of `dummy_img`. Re-enables OSNet ReID for the first time. **Impact:** High — foundational for proposals 4 and 6. **Effort:** S-M (one line of code, but queue-memory implications need a look). **Retraining:** None. **Stages:** 5; unblocks 10.

2. **⭐ Use `side_score` as a Front/Back prior.** In `_resolve_session_tracks`, prefer the track with the higher `side_score` as Front; allow the textiness margin (`_SESSION_TEXTINESS_MARGIN = 0.03`, already defined at `pipeline.py:51`) to override the pHash gate when the two disagree. **Why:** The correct invariant, already computed, currently ignored. **Impact:** High. **Effort:** S. **Retraining:** None (optionally re-tune the margin). **Stages:** 8.

3. **Quality-weighted primary-track selection.** Replace the sort-by-length with `(0.6 · normalized_length + 0.4 · mean_quality_score)`. **Why:** The quality score (Stage 7) is the signal that resolves "long but blurry vs short but sharp". **Impact:** Med-High. **Effort:** S. **Retraining:** None. **Stages:** 8.

4. **Embedding-based "same physical card" criterion.** After proposal #1, replace the pHash gate with cosine of real-frame OSNet embeddings (front-track representative vs. candidate-track representative). Calibrate threshold against the regression set's labeled same-card pairs. **Why:** pHash on the inner 60% is content-blind to perspective change; OSNet was trained to be view-invariant and should outperform pHash on this comparison. **Impact:** High. **Effort:** M (blocked by #1). **Retraining:** None initially; optional fine-tune on cards.sqlite cross-track pairs. **Stages:** 8, 10.

5. **Cross-video dedup via embedding.** Stage 10's `is_reid_duplicate` becomes useful after #1. Use pHash as a cheap pre-filter (broad candidates) and ReID cosine as the deciding metric. **Why:** pHash collisions are common across TCG sets that share a border template (different cards, near-identical contour); embeddings discriminate. **Impact:** Med. **Effort:** S (after #1). **Retraining:** None. **Stages:** 10.

### Risks & open questions
- OSNet's training prior is person-ReID (MSMT17), not card-ReID. Empirically the embeddings should still be discriminative for cards (plenty of texture signal), but this is an off-distribution use. The right verification is a small `tests/regression/` measurement: cosine distances on labeled same-card vs different-card pairs from `cards.sqlite`. If the distributions don't separate cleanly, fall back to a hand-crafted feature (e.g., color histogram + dHash + textiness) before considering a learned head as a future addition.
- The Front/Back assignment after this rework relies on `side_score` (textiness) alone for the angle label, plus `embedding-distance` for "same physical card" linking. Failure mode to watch: cards with high-texture *backs* (foiled or art-laden backs, common in modern Pokémon) confuse `side_score`. If we see this in regression, the right next step is broadening `side_score` to a multi-signal heuristic (edge density, color variance, pHash entropy of the inner DCT block) — still no new model.
- Passing source frames through the producer/consumer queue increases the per-item memory footprint substantially (a 4K frame is ~25 MB). The pipeline's `queue_size = 256` may need to shrink, or the frame may need to be referenced by file path rather than carried in-memory — the producer already persists source frames at `_persist_source_frame` (`pipeline.py:_persist_source_frame`), so path-references are free.

## 5. Theme — Calibration and data flywheel

### Failure mode

Material thresholds in `pipeline.py` are all hand-tuned constants with no recorded calibration:

```
NoveltyGate threshold                = 0.08
_SAME_APPEARANCE_HAMMING_MAX         = 8
_SESSION_DUPLICATE_HAMMING_MAX       = 6
_SAME_CARD_HAMMING_MAX               = 22
_SESSION_TEXTINESS_MARGIN            = 0.03
_SESSION_APPEARANCE_SIMILARITY_MIN   = 0.995
_SESSION_MERGE_SIMILARITY_MIN        = 0.99
QualityScore weights                 = [0.25, 0.15, 0.15, 0.10, 0.10, 0.20, 0.05]
valley_drop_ratio                    = 0.40
centroid_jump_ratio                  = 0.30
```

The regression infrastructure at `tests/regression/` (`truth.py`, `pipeline_runner.py`, `metrics.py`, `test_truth.py`, `test_matcher.py`, `test_cross_video.py`, `report.py`) clearly contemplates ground-truth-based evaluation, but it isn't tied to threshold tuning — there's no closed loop between "this threshold changed" and "this metric moved by X".

### Proposals

1. **Define a robustness metric pack** computed on the regression corpus. Five metrics:
   - **Card recall** — fraction of ground-truth cards that produced ≥1 canonical entry.
   - **Card precision** — fraction of canonical entries matching a ground-truth card (1 − FPR).
   - **Front/Back F1** — correctness of the angle label per matched card.
   - **Multi-card scene survival** — card recall restricted to videos tagged multi-card / rapid-swap.
   - **Foil survival** — card recall + fusion-quality on cards tagged foil/holo.

   **Why:** Threshold sweeps require a small fixed metric vector to optimize against. Run-telemetry is not the same thing. **Impact:** High (foundational for proposals 2 and 4 in this theme). **Effort:** M (most pieces exist in `tests/regression/`; need to extend truth-tag schema with scene-type and foil labels). **Retraining:** None. **Stages:** harness / CI.

2. **Threshold auto-sweep harness.** `scripts/calibrate.py` that runs the regression pack across a grid of (`NoveltyGate threshold`, `_SAME_CARD_HAMMING_MAX`, `valley_drop_ratio`, `centroid_jump_ratio`) and emits a Pareto front of precision/recall. Human picks the operating point. **Why:** Hand-tuned defaults are likely good but neither optimal nor recorded with their tradeoffs. **Impact:** Med (each parameter is worth ~1-3% per metric; in aggregate several percentage points). **Effort:** M (depends on regression-corpus runtime; offline / overnight is fine). **Retraining:** None. **Stages:** harness.

3. **Hard-case capture for active learning.** On every pipeline run, append to `hard_cases.jsonl` any session that:
   - resolves to more than 2 distinct "Front" tracks (missed swap),
   - has a Front-vs-other Hamming margin within 4 of `_SAME_CARD_HAMMING_MAX` (borderline),
   - keeps a detection with confidence ∈ [0.45, 0.55] (just-above-threshold),
   - has best-canonical `border_purity < 0.3` (likely occlusion the term didn't catch).

   **Why:** Grows a labeled queue at the rate of real ambiguity, monotonically, without manual sampling. Surfaces the per-video distribution tails that motivate the adaptive-threshold work in #23, and provides the ground for any future learned head should the algorithmic path fall short. **Impact:** Med (compounds over months). **Effort:** S. **Stages:** cross-cutting (persisted via `storage.py`).

4. **Per-video adaptive thresholds** where evidence supports. `tracker_t_high` and `adaptive_gap_recommended` already adapt per-video. Extend the same pattern to: novelty threshold (Otsu on the in-video candidate-novelty distribution), Front/Back Hamming threshold (intra-track Hamming distance percentile). **Why:** Per-video adaptation works where we've tried it; extending it removes more hand-tuning. **Impact:** Med. **Effort:** M. **Retraining:** None. **Stages:** 4, 8, 10.

### Risks & open questions
- The metric pack is only as good as the truth labels. `tests/regression/truth.py` needs an audit before being load-bearing; partial or stale labels will mislead the auto-sweep.
- Hard-case capture grows monotonically; a retention policy or compaction strategy is needed before the file becomes unmanageable.
- Auto-sweep can over-fit to the regression corpus. Hold-out videos and a manual confirmation step are non-negotiable.

### Recommended sequencing

**Wave 1 — independent S-effort fixes** (proposals #1-8). All eight can be done in parallel by different contributors or as a single batch. No dependencies, no calibration prerequisites. The pipeline emerges with real ReID restored, the textiness signal in use, the Front/Back gate quality-aware, color information in the novelty gate, two tracker heuristics fixed, and a refreshed BG model. This is the baseline against which subsequent waves are measured.

**Wave 2 — foundational M-effort work** (proposals #9-14). One foundational item — the robustness metric pack (#9) — unblocks the calibration work in Wave 3. Per-pixel BG (#10), oriented-IoU (#11), ECC re-registration (#12), per-region valley (#13), and per-tile occlusion residual (#14) are independent of each other and of #9 — they ship when their author has bandwidth. After this wave the pipeline has a measurable robustness baseline and most of the algorithmic upgrades in place.

**Wave 3 — leverage on foundations** (proposals #15-19). Two embedding-driven fixes that depend on #1 (#15, #16). Three calibration items (#17, #18, #19) where #18 and #19 depend on the metric pack (#9). Most of the wave is S effort once its prerequisites land; the value comes from composing prior work.

**Wave 4 — discretionary** (proposal #20). Foil-aware fusion is M effort and benefits foil-card quality specifically. Defer until regression metrics show foil-survival is the bottleneck.

## 7. Open questions for the spec / plan phase

1. **Frame-passing path for ReID.** Plumb frames through the existing `frame_queue` (memory cost) or pass image-path references and re-decode in the tracker (CPU cost). The producer already persists detection source frames via `_persist_source_frame`, so the path-reference route is essentially free; needs a measurement to confirm re-decode latency doesn't dominate.
2. **Truth-label schema.** What's in `tests/regression/truth.py` today, and does it carry per-card per-frame annotations or just video-level outcomes? Determines how much new labeling is needed for the metric pack (#9) and what's already available for verifying the embedding-distance change (#15).
3. **Scene and foil tagging.** Whether multi-card / rapid-swap / foil labels are added to the truth schema as part of this audit (informs #13, #14, #20) or deferred.
4. **Per-video adaptation vs. fixed defaults.** For each threshold that becomes adaptive (#19), do we keep the fixed default as a fallback for short videos with insufficient distribution data?
5. **Single-pass sampler scope.** §8.4 names the two-pass sampler as the dominant blocker to a real-time pipeline. Is replacing it part of this audit, a parallel workstream, or deferred until robustness is settled?

## 8. Performance footprint

The v4.1 pipeline runs as two cooperating Python processes connected by bounded queues. This section summarizes where each component runs, what it costs, how much memory it uses, and how the proposals in §6 change that picture. The forward goal of single-pass / real-time operation is addressed in §8.4.

### 8.1 Process and device layout

Stages 1-3 (sampler, triage, corner detection) run in a producer subprocess; Stages 4 onward (novelty gate, tracking, refinement, scoring, fusion, dedup, storage) run in the main process. The producer/main split decouples ffmpeg + MPS startup from the orchestration loop.

| Component | Process | Device | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Video decode | Producer | CPU (HW-accel on macOS via VideoToolbox; decord on CUDA) | `reader_backend = "auto"` |
| Pass 1 scan metrics (Sobel, edges, empty-ratio) | Producer | GPU (auto) | `gpu_utils.compute_presence_metrics_batched`, 192-px proxy |
| Presence classifier (MobileNetV3-Small) | Producer | auto: MPS → CUDA → CPU | `chunk_size = 32`, 224×224 input |

The dominant compute cost is the YOLOv8-OBB forward pass (Producer, GPU). The dominant memory cost is the bounded frame queue (between processes, RAM). Everything else is small.

### 8.2 Memory footprint

Approximate steady-state usage at 1080p capture; numbers scale linearly with resolution.

- **`frame_queue`** (bounded at 256 entries). Each `FramePacket` carries the full-res frame `(H, W, 3) uint8` plus thin metadata. At 1080p the worst-case footprint is ~6 MB × 256 ≈ 1.5 GB; typical steady-state holds 10–50 frames (60–300 MB).
- **`detection_queue`** (bounded). Each `DetectionPacket` carries detection metadata only (no frame); negligible.
- **`BackgroundModel.gray`** — `(H, W) float32` — ~8 MB at 1080p, ~24 MB at 4K. Doubles after Proposal #10 (per-pixel mean + variance).
- **Persisted source frames** — one JPEG per detection-bearing frame on disk (~150–500 KB each). Read on demand.
- **Model weights** (loaded once at startup):
  - YOLOv8-OBB (`cardcaptor_v3_best.pt`): ~50 MB on device
  - MobileNetV3-Small presence classifier: ~10 MB on device
  - OSNet x0_25 ReID: ~3 MB on device
- **Per-track in-flight state**: `TrackState` (instance UUID, candidates list, last centroid, ReID embedding ~512 floats, optional appearance vector) — ~10–50 KB per active track.
- **SQLite `cards.sqlite`** — grows with capture history; not in RAM.

**Total RAM steady-state (1080p, typical operation):** roughly 500 MB – 2 GB across the two processes, dominated by `frame_queue` and model weights. The model weights load once on device (MPS or CUDA); the queue lives in shared host memory.

These are estimates derived from the architecture; actual figures should be read from `run_telemetry.json` which records measured stage timings and counts.

### 8.4 Net effect and real-time outlook

**Added per-frame cost (sum of streaming-compatible rows, typical 1–3 active tracks):**

- Real ReID inference: ~3 ms (per detection; ~1–2 ms amortized per frame at typical detection density)
- Lab color cvtColor: <1 ms
- Per-pixel BG EWMA update: ~1–2 ms (gated frames only)
- Spatial glare: ~1 ms (per scored crop, not per frame)
- Per-region valley (producer): ~1 ms per scan frame
- Per-tile occlusion residual: ~2–6 ms (scales with active tracks)
- Miscellaneous (OBB centroid, online percentiles, etc.): <1 ms

**Total per-frame addition: roughly 5–10 ms beyond v4.1 baseline**, distributed across producer (~1 ms) and main process (~5–9 ms).

**Memory addition: roughly +20–40 MB** total (per-pixel Lab BG model + per-track tile state), negligible against the ~500 MB – 2 GB v4.1 baseline.

**Where the budget lives.** v4.1's per-detection-frame wall time is dominated by the YOLOv8-OBB forward pass at 640 px (~25–40 ms on Apple Silicon MPS, less on CUDA). At 30 fps the per-frame budget is ~33 ms; v4.1 is comfortably real-time-capable on CUDA today and runs at ~15 fps on MPS in offline batch mode. After this spec's Waves 1-2 land, the per-frame budget is unchanged in character: detector inference still dominates, additions are bounded. Real-time deployment will still need to either lower `detection_width` (640 → 416 / 320) or accept a 15 fps target on MPS; neither is a change this spec introduces.

**Three places v4.1 is batch-oriented**, in decreasing severity:

1. **Two-pass sampler (Stage 1).** Pass 1 walks the entire video at `fast_scan_fps = 15.0` to find presence windows; Pass 2 decodes selected frames at full resolution. **This is the hardest blocker to real-time.** The replacement is a single-pass sampler that scores presence online and emits candidates as windows close. The existing `PresenceClassifier` runs frame-by-frame; what needs to change is the window-management and full-res decode logic. This is not a proposal in this document.
2. **Background model construction.** v4.1 builds `BackgroundModel.gray` once from a heap of low-presence-score sampler proxies. Proposal #10 (per-pixel BG with variance) is streaming-compatible by design — its EWMA update path runs per frame during quiescent episodes. Proposal #7 (periodic refresh) is the lighter-weight precursor.
3. **Session resolution.** Tracks are resolved into Front/Back and persisted at video-end. Streaming variant: close a session N seconds after the last detection within it. Introduces a session-close latency budget; does not delay the pipeline as a whole.

**Forward incompatibilities this audit deliberately avoids:**

- **Whole-video aggregations** beyond what already exists. Per-video p50/p95 calculations are tolerable if replaced with online estimators (Proposal #19 must use one); whole-video sorts or full-corpus model fits are not.
- **Two-pass video reads** beyond the existing sampler. The proposals above use a single read of each frame.
- **Synchronous external dependencies in the per-frame path.** Nothing in this document calls out to a network service, a database query, or a disk-bound operation per frame. The only per-frame file I/O is the source-frame persist that v4.1 already does.
